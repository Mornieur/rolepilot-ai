# Product rules

Job actions are scoped to a profile + job pair. `new` is the absence of an explicit decision; `saved`, `ignored`, `applied`, and `rejected` are persisted decisions. Any manual transition is permitted.

Actions do not trigger AI, notifications, learning, analytics, or an automatic application. Application artifacts will always be reviewed by a person. Future metrics and insights must distinguish observed data, user preferences, AI interpretation, and recommendation.

See [job lifecycle](JOB_LIFECYCLE.md) and [application assistance](APPLICATION_ASSISTANCE.md).
